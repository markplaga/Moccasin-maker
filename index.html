Size from a photo

Print the measuring mat, put your foot on it, and take a photo from straight above. Then tap the points the tool asks for.

Print the mat. Print at 100% (actual size). Check the 5 cm square with a ruler.
Trace or stand. For the most accurate result, stand on the mat with your heel on the heel line and trace around your foot with a pencil held upright. Or just stand on it.
Photograph. Hold the phone flat, about waist height, directly over the mat. All four corner targets must be visible.
Tap. Tap corners A to D, then the heel, toe and widths. Tracing the outline is optional and gives the pattern your own foot shape.
Or drag a photo onto the box below, or paste one.
